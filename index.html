“Celebrating a Silver Legacy of Unity.”

Warm greetings from Home Links Family Club.

It gives us immense pleasure to reach out to you as Home Links prepares to celebrate its Silver Jubilee (25th Anniversary) in 2027.

Founded with the vision of strengthening family bonds and fostering friendship, unity, and mutual support among members of the Elur Chetty Community, Home Links has, over the past twenty-five years, grown into a vibrant family club.

Through cultural, educational, social, and community-centered activities, the club has brought together generations while preserving our rich heritage and inspiring the younger generation.

“A celebration of knowledge, community and the bonds that unite us.”

SILVER JUBILEE CELEBRATIONSThe Competition

As one of the flagship events of our Silver Jubilee celebrations, we are delighted to organize the All India Silver Jubilee Quiz Competition, exclusively for members of the Elur Chetty Community from across India.

The competition aims to promote knowledge, encourage healthy competition, strengthen community bonds, and provide a common platform for participants to interact and celebrate our shared identity.
